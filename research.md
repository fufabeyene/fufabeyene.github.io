My research areas are enumerative and algebraic combinatorics. Specifically, I study the distribution of various statistics on combinatorial objects like set partitions, permutations, and subexcedant functions. Also, I study the structure of different combinatorial objects.

My Ph.D. Dissertation, defended in January 2023, was about some combinatorial results on set partitions and permutations using subexcedant functions. My Supervisor was [Roberto Mantaci](http://www.informatique.univ-paris-diderot.fr/), my cosupervisors were [Samuel A. Fufa](https://www.aau.edu.et) and [Jörgen Backelin](https://www.su.se/joeb).
